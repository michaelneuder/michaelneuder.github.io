- specific climbs
  + video. "Realization / Biographie" (2001, 5.15a)
    Chris Sharma climbing the first ever 5.15a in 2001. Iconic. All time. Hard not to love Chris.
  + video. "Silence" (2017, 5.15d)
    Adam Ondra on Silence in Flathanger Cave. First 5.15d. Has the legendary upside-down toe jam move. Will Bosi thinks this boulder problem (in the middle of the route) is a V16.
  + video. "Dreamcatcher" (2005, 5.14d)
    Chris Sharma on an absurdly pretty natural line. I remember watching this as a kid on repeat. The only climb on this list that I have seen in person.
  + video. "Witness the Fitness" (2005, V15)
    Chris Sharma on somewhat random boulder that was very inspiring to me as a kid. I thought it was so cool that the roof was that long and it was still a boulder.
  + video. "Duality of Man" (2025, 5.15d)
    An amazing piece of filmography. The music is unbelievable and synced up with the climbing. Sean Bailey seems like an extremely cool dude and it was interesting to hear him talk about sponsorships and the financial reality of being a pro climber.  
  + video. "Change" (2012, 5.15c)
    Ondra on the first 5.15c. Classic young Adam.
  + video. "DNA (live stream)" (2026, 5.15d)
    Jakub Schubert live streaming his send of DNA. I was super impressed that he committed to doing live stream attempts. It ramps up the pressure so much, but he just lives for that moment (probably why he is/was such a good comp climber) and delivered!
  + video. "Midnight Way" (2026, 5.15a)
    Sonnie Trotter on a wild adventure send in Squamish.
  + video. "Cobra Crack" (2006, 5.14b)
    Sonnie Trotter FA of one of the prettiest natural crack lines in the world. Old school and sick.
