- specific climbs
  + video. "Realization / Biographie" (2001, 5.15a)
    Chris Sharma climbing the first ever 5.15a in 2001. Iconic. All time. Hard not to love Chris.
  + video. "Silence" (2017, 5.15d)
    Adam Ondra on Silence in Flathanger Cave. First 5.15d. Has the legendary upside-down toe jam move. Will Bosi thinks this boulder problem (in the middle of the route) is a V16.
  + video. "Dreamcatcher" (2005, 5.14d)
    Chris Sharma on an absurdly pretty natural line. I remember watching this as a kid on repeat. The only climb on this list that I have seen in person.
  + video. "Witness the Fitness" (2005, V15)
    Chris Sharma on somewhat random boulder that was very inspiring to me as a kid. I thought it was so cool that the roof was that long and it was still a boulder.
  + video. "Duality of Man" (2025, 5.15d)
    Sean Bailey. An amazing piece of filmography. The music is unbelievable and synced up with the climbing. Sean seems like an extremely cool dude and it was interesting to hear him talk about sponsorships and the financial reality of being a pro climber.  
  + video. "Change" (2012, 5.15c)
    Ondra on the first 5.15c. Classic young Adam.
  + video. "Suprême Jumbo Love" (2022, 5.15c)
    Seb Bouin doing the direct start to Jumbo Love. One of the more absurd endurance lines you will ever see.
  + video. "Alphane" (2022, v17)
    Shawn Raboutou. Amazing edit (really enjoy things directed by Ben Neilson). Great music. Captures the bouldering vibe well. And Daniel Woods is still crushing!
  + video. "DNA (live stream)" (2026, 5.15d)
    Jakub Schubert live streaming his send of DNA. I was super impressed that he committed to doing live stream attempts. It ramps up the pressure so much, but he just lives for that moment (probably why he is/was such a good comp climber) and delivered!
  + video. "Midnight Way" (2026, 5.15a)
    Sonnie Trotter on a wild adventure send in Squamish.
  + video. "Cobra Crack" (2006, 5.14b)
    Sonnie Trotter FA of one of the prettiest natural crack lines in the world. Old school and sick.

- feature films
  + imdb. "The Alpinist" (2021)
    Marc-André Leclerc. Probably my favorite piece of climbing content ever. Marc was truly from a different time. Obviously an insanely tragic story, but a beautiful homage to a life well lived and a love lost.
  + imdb. "The Dark Wizard" (2026)
    Dean Potter. A masterful, long-form look at Dean's life, art, spirituality, ego, and darkness. Some of the scenes in this film gave me chills. But it is a great story about one of the biggest icons of his era of Yosemite climbing.
  + video. "Rampage" (1999)
    Probably the most influential climbing film ever made. It has an absurdly cool vibe and it is fun to see Chris when he is so young. Obi and others are also iconic. The film is pure energy.
  + imdb. "Meru" (2015)
    Conrad Anker, Jimmy Chin, Renan Ozturk. An epic adventure story. This is hardcore alpinism and big mountaineering at its finest. The decision to go back up with Renan is kind of insane to me still, but I guess that is what it takes to push things to the edge.
  + imdb. "Free Solo" (2018)
    Alex Honnold. It is epic and the cinematography is legendary.
  + imdb. "The Dawn Wall" (2017)
    Tommy Caldwell and Kevin Jorgenson. A great story of team work and mental perseverance. Also interesting that they chose the specific style they did of each person having to cleanly lead each pitch (rather than the standard "team free").
  
- reel rock
  + "Safety Third" (Reel Rock 12, 2017)
    Brad Gobright. Maybe the most relatable pro climber in history and truly captured the risk-taking, adrenaline-seeking feeling. His free solo of Hairstyles and Attitudes is almost difficult to watch because it is so bold.
  + "The Cobra & the Heart" (Reel Rock 19, 2025)
    An unbelievable story of love and forgiveness. Thomasina is an incredible person.
  + video 1, video 2, video 3. "The Nose Speed Record" (Reel Rock 14, 2019)
    Iconic duos and spectacular risks. "No gear, no fall." "Brad just monos the bolt hangers."
  + video. "Sketchy Andy" (Reel Rock 6, 2011)
    One of the most memorable reel rocks from when I was young. I thought Andy was so free and so inspiring. RIP.
  + video. "The Swiss Machine" (Reel Rock 5, 2010)
    Another all time reel rock. The scene of Ueli running through the snow on the final summit ridge with "Welcome Home" playing as the drone zooms out it permanently etched into my mind. RIP.
  + video. "La Dura Dura" (Reel Rock 7, 2012)
    An awesome moment of passing the torch from Sharma to Ondra. They are such different people with such different approaches! I remember watching this and rooting for Sharma. But the time had come and it was the Age of Ondra.
  + "Alone on the Torre" (Reel Rock 20, 2026)
    Collin Haley does some of the most ridiculous solo-mountaineering imaginable. A must watch if you are into Patagonia epics.
  + "Janja on Bibliographie" (Reel Rock 20, 2026)
    Hilarious and light-hearted. Fun to see Janja have to work so hard and her relationship with her coach. Bummed that they didn't credit Brooke as the first woman to send 5.15c though.
  + video. "Wide Boyz" (Reel Rock 7, 2012)
    Hard not to love Tom and Pete. But how on earth did they expect that the pink point would be accepted by the climbing community. Glad they went back to get the redpoint.
  + video. "DNA" (Reel Rock 17, 2023)
    Seb Bouin's mega line. Epic story and love his deep roots to climbing in France. His mom seems strong too.
  + video 1, video 2. "The High Road" (Reel Rock 14, 2019)
    Nina Williams doing some of the sketchiest highballs. She is so solid though. Never a doubt.
  + video 1, video 2. "King Lines" (Reel Rock 2, 2007)
    Classic vintage Sharma doing deep water solos. What is there not to love.
  + video. "Origins: Obe & Ashima" (Reel Rock 6, 2011)
    A fun story of coach and student. I remember seeing Ashima warming up at my climbing gym in Colorado Springs before ABS bouldering nationals one year. She was so small.
  + "Hold On, Pain Ends" (Reel Rock 20, 2026)
    Ben Mayforth. A gritty story of mental and physical health. Really good.
  + video 1, video 2, video 3. "Age of Ondra" (Reel Rock 13, 2018)
    Cool to get into Ondra's head. Probably the most interesting climb was Disbelief in Canmore, Canada, because it is the first time I have ever seen Ondra actually look like he was struggling mentally.
  + "Sending Spree" (Reel Rock 20, 2026)
    Michaela Kiersch. Simple premise: she sends everything.
  + "Honnold 3.0" (Reel Rock 7, 2012)
    Honnold. Too Big To Flail was the most memorable for me.
  + video 1, video 2. "Riders on the Storm" (Reel Rock 19, 2025)
    Fun epic adventure in Patagonia.
  + video 1, video 2. "Mission Antarctica" (Reel Rock non-tour, 2019)
    Absurd wind surfing to get to remote peaks in Antarctica. 
  + video. "Sleeping Lion" (Reel Rock non-tour, 2023)
    Chris Sharma sending hard as an old guy. Hilarious clip of him at a computer trying to do business stuff. Family man Sharma is epic.  
  + video 1, video 2. "Burning the Flame" (Reel Rock 17, 2023)
    Cool free climbing on Trango Towers. Dealing with crazy adverse conditions.
